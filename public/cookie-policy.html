2. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

2.1. Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, enhance user experience, and provide information to site owners.

2.2. Similar Technologies: This Policy also covers:

  • Web beacons / pixel tags: Tiny images embedded in web pages or emails to track activity.
  • Local storage: Data stored in your browser (e.g., HTML5 localStorage, sessionStorage).
  • Fingerprinting: Techniques that identify your device based on its configuration.

5. CONSENT MECHANISM

  • (c) Customize Preferences — choose which categories of optional cookies to enable or disable.

5.2. You can change your cookie preferences at any time by:

  • (a) Clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in the Website footer.
  • (b) Clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the Website (the consent banner will reappear).

5.3. Consent is not required for strictly necessary cookies, as they are essential for the Website to function.

5.4. GDPR / UK GDPR Compliance: For users in the EU/UK, no optional cookies will be placed until explicit consent is provided (opt-in model).

5.5. DPDP Act / Indian Law Compliance: Cookie consent is obtained as part of the broader consent mechanism described in our Privacy Policy (CIXIO-PP-001).

5.6. CCPA / CPRA Compliance: California residents may opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information collected via cookies. See our Privacy Policy for details.
