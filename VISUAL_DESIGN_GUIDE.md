## Color Palette

### Primary Colors
- **Primary Blue:** #0066FF (Electric blue)
- **Secondary Cyan:** #00D9FF (Bright cyan)
- **Dark Background:** #0A0E27 (Deep navy)
- **Dark Alt:** #1A1F3A (Indigo)

### Gradients
- **Primary Gradient:** #0066FF → #00D9FF (135deg)
- **Background:** #0A0E27 → #1A1F3A (135deg)
- **Glow Effects:** Multiple layers with varying opacity

### Neutral Colors
- **White:** #FFFFFF (Text, cards)
- **Light Gray:** #F8F9FA (Backgrounds)
- **Medium Gray:** #BFC0C0 (Secondary text)
- **Dark Gray:** #2D3142 (Text on light)

## Animation Timings

### Entrance Animations
- **Hero Title:** 1s ease-out, delay 0.2s
- **Hero Subtitle:** 1s ease-out, delay 0.4s
- **Hero CTA:** 1s ease-out, delay 0.6s
- **Hero Stats:** 1s ease-out, delay 0.8s

### Hover Transitions
- **Cards:** 0.4s cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)
- **Buttons:** 0.3s ease
- **Icons:** 0.4s ease

### Special Effects
- **Gradient Flow:** 15s ease infinite
- **Particle Animation:** 20s ease-in-out infinite
- **Float Animation:** 3s ease-in-out infinite
- **Pulse:** 2s ease-in-out infinite
